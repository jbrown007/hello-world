Ideal Seventeen, Priced

Every ideal pick against last season's full-PPR total and the 2026 web consensus (ESPN Clay/Yates, FantasyPros, PFN — gathered Sept 4, tagged est where derived). The point of this sheet is the right-hand column: the edge cases.

Edge case No. 1 — found & fixed

Jayden Higgins is OUT for the season (torn ACL in the Raiders joint practice, IR). He was your R13 buy. This cross-check caught it, the boards and the printed sheet are corrected, and R13 is now Jordyn Tyson (same W8 slot, partition unchanged).

Edge case No. 2 — Downs got company

The Colts signed Keenan Allen (1yr, ~$8M; 22.4% target share last season). The +15 value gap that made Downs a lock is thinner — hold the R8-9 window but don't reach; if his price sags past 92, he's a better pick at 101.

Edge case No. 3 — consensus likes Loveland

The web projects Loveland TE3 above Warren TE4 (~260 vs ~227 est PPR). Warren still wins at this seat: he's on the board at 53 (Loveland's TE3 price isn't), and Loveland unravels the Bears cap + W10 with Caleb. Talent call: closer than the old sheet said. Structure call: unchanged.

Edge case No. 4 — the kicker fallback was wrong

If Dicker (W7) is gone at 197 — likely, the room took him at 151 last year — the listed fallback Fairbairn is W8, which this roster fills at cap (Black + Tyson). The right fallback is Cam Little (JAX, W7). Caught by this sheet's partition column.
