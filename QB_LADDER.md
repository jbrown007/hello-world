post-draft wire with nothing startable. **QB supply in this league is fixed
at the draft.** Rostered arms are the only in-season QB currency.

## QB3 selection test (ADOPTED)

The 2025 comparable: Goff (9.05), Ward (9.07), Geno (9.10), Lawrence (9.11) -
starting NFL quarterbacks inside eleven picks at a tenth of a R2 price. And
the disqualifier from our own 2025 draft: a rookie stash (Dart, 15.08) cannot
be traded to a manager whose starter just went down - the buyer wants points
this week. All three required:

1. **Named Week 1 starter**, not a competition winner-in-waiting.
   (Cam Ward and Caleb Williams both pass; they are the candidate list.)
2. **Bye different from QB1 and QB2** (triangulation - already enforced).
3. **Acquired R9-13, targeting the R9-10 tier.** Paying R7-8 is paying
   starter prices for a bench asset.

Sell window: Weeks 4-10. The Dec 4 deadline means the market runs nearly all
season, but buyers stop paying late. Shaun DeNiro (thin QB2) is the mapped
customer; Da LockDownGoon and the four-QB veterans are competitors.

## QB4 at the draft: REJECTED (the math, not the idea)

Confirmed roster: 17 picks = 3 QB + 6 RB + 5 WR + 1 TE + K + DST, slack zero
(ROSTER_LEDGER, binding per the 8/1 reconciliation). Four QBs + TE + K + DST
= 7 spots, leaving 10 for RB/WR against an 11-spot floor. **A drafted QB4
requires breaking the 6/5 floor to 5/5 - the absolute minimum - in a
zero-IR league where RB attrition is the thing that actually breaks.**
The RB floor exists because of the 2025 finish; it does not bend for a
flier.

If Josh ever wants to overrule this (5 RB / 5 WR / 4 QB), that is a
deliberate strategy decision to make BEFORE draft day, not a table
audible. As of now: not taken.

## QB4 in-season: ADOPTED as the churn doctrine

The ladder survives as data/lessons.yaml doctrine: when byes approach or a
buyer's starter goes down, churn the last bench spot toward a fourth arm.
Acceptable profiles only: (a) a starter who fell for price reasons, (b) a
backup with a genuine path. Exit rule: **QB4 is the first drop** when RB/WR
attrition demands a spot - the single exception is an active negotiation
week. An arm you will neither trade nor drop is a dead spot with a story.
